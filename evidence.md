# Evidence and sources

[Wiki home](README.md) · [Techniques](techniques.md) · [Codex coaching playbook](codex-coach.md)

Research checked November 14, 2026. This is a focused narrative review. Searches covered retention and transfer, learning techniques, expertise, motor and perceptual learning, guitar and music, writing, math, programming, soccer, and AI tutoring. The wiki draws on meta-analyses, systematic reviews, research syntheses, and studies where they clarify what a technique looks like or where it breaks down. The practice plans apply that evidence to adult self-study. Many underlying studies come from school classrooms, laboratories, or groups with prior training.

The [mastery research section](mastery/index.md) adds topic-specific evidence on expertise, practice design and feedback, adaptation and pressure, and retention after nonuse. Those pages link directly to the research used for each claim.

## How much confidence to put in each recommendation

| Recommendation | Confidence and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Recall previously studied material and check it | **Strong for retention.** Extensive experimental and classroom evidence, especially relative to restudying. |
| Space practice over time | **Strong for retaining verbal material.** Useful intervals depend on the goal and task. Evidence also supports spacing in math, with more variable results for motor tasks and different kinds of breaks. |
| Start with an example and fade support when a learner is stuck | **Supported for novice learning of structured procedures.** There is direct math evidence and narrower evidence in programming. The amount of help changes as learners gain prior knowledge. |
| Interleave tasks that require discriminating among approaches | **Supported for some tasks, including math.** Effects vary across materials and according to what was compared. |
| Self-explain and compare cases | **Supported with relevant prompts and examples.** The design of the activity and the learner’s background matter. |
| Give feedback that identifies a usable next action | **Supported on average.** Effects depend on the information, the task, and what the learner can do with it. |
| Mix focused repetitions with realistic performance and measure transfer | **Supported as a design principle.** Direct evidence about optimal practice mixes and schedules is narrower. The wiki presents example plans for each domain. |
| Use AI to coach and preserve independent practice | **Emerging and context-dependent.** Results vary by tutor design, group, subject, and test. |

See the bibliography by topic below. These confidence labels apply to the outcome and scope described in each row. They are qualitative judgments, and they do not compare the magnitude of effects across techniques.

## Limitations by technique

- **Study outcomes differ.** An immediate quiz, a final exam, a timed drill, and a performance with a live opponent answer different questions. Report the outcome and when it was measured. [Soderstrom and Bjork, learning versus performance](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691615569000)
- **The comparison matters.** Retrieval often beats rereading on retention. A 2025 meta-analysis found a small pooled advantage when it was compared with active elaborative methods. Some individual comparisons were inconclusive. Provide corrective feedback when the learner makes an error. [Gonçalves et al., retrieval versus elaboration](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10076-6)
- **Effect sizes answer different questions.** Ruitenburg and colleagues pooled 53 studies in primary and secondary classrooms. Across different eligible datasets and comparisons, reported average standardized effects were 0.37 for retrieval, 0.20 for distributed practice, and 0.40 for interleaving. Subgroup evidence was sometimes sparse. Retrieval benefits were clearer for factual knowledge and retention than for some other outcomes in that analysis. Treat the estimates as results for their respective comparisons. [Ruitenburg et al., classroom meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10225-5)
- **Interleaving in motor learning is sensitive to setting and design.** A motor-transfer meta-analysis found clearer advantages of random practice in lab studies. The estimate for applied studies was statistically uncertain. Another review using change scores reported no reliable overall advantage in sport and physical education. Alternating among tasks is a choice to evaluate on an independent retest. [Czyż et al., motor transfer](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2024.1377122) · [Ammar et al., skilled motor performance](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-024-09892-z)
- **Spacing studies use different timescales.** A review describes benefits from distributing practice over days for some complex and motor skills. Two experiments published in 2026 found no clear benefit from longer or more frequent brief rests in a motor-sequence session once the test conditions were matched. These comparisons address different scheduling questions. [Smith and Scarf, long-timescale spacing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5476736/) · [Gupta and Rickard, motor rest breaks](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42177224/)
- **Motor cueing and feedback frequency remain uncertain.** Chua and colleagues found better average results for cues about the movement effect. A later bias-adjusted analysis found much smaller estimated advantages. Research on feedback frequency also leaves an optimal dosage unclear. [Chua et al., attentional focus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34843301/) · [McKay et al., reporting bias and attentional focus](https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000451) · [McKay et al., feedback frequency](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psychsport.2022.102165)
- **Expertise evidence involves measurement choices.** The original deliberate-practice framework emphasized structured improvement activities, often designed by a teacher. Cross-domain results vary and are largely associations. Authors disagree on which practice to count. A violin replication illustrates the uncertainty around how practice hours distinguish top performers. There is no established universal hour threshold for mastery. [Ericsson et al.](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.100.3.363) · [Macnamara et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24986855/) · [Ericsson and Harwell](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.02396) · [Macnamara and Maitra](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6731745/)
- **Learning preferences and access needs affect choices.** Provide usable formats and select representations for the task. People may prefer a presentation mode. Evidence that matching instruction to a person’s assigned visual or auditory learning style reliably improves learning remains weak. [Pashler et al., learning styles review](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x) · [Clinton-Lisell and Litzinger, meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11270031/)
- **The precise practice dose is usually open.** A 25-minute session, four-week plan, review gap, or tempo threshold in this wiki is an example. Set the review date to match the retention goal and adjust the next session based on the learner’s attempt.

## Annotated bibliography

### General learning and memory

- **National Academies (2018), knowledge and reasoning.** Consensus synthesis on prior knowledge, expertise, and learning strategies. [How People Learn II, chapter 5](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/24783/chapter/7)
- **National Research Council (2000), learning and transfer.** Discusses the roles of context, understanding, prior experience, and different transfer measures. [How People Learn, chapter 3](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/9853/chapter/6)
- **Dunlosky et al. (2013).** Comparative review of ten techniques across study materials, learner characteristics, and tests. [Improving Students’ Learning With Effective Learning Techniques](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26173288/)
- **Soderstrom and Bjork (2015).** Reviews evidence that practice performance can diverge from durable learning. [Learning Versus Performance](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691615569000)
- **Agarwal et al. (2021) and Yang et al. (2021).** Review and meta-analysis of retrieval practice in educational settings. [Agarwal et al., applied review](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09595-9) · [Yang et al., classroom meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33683913/)
- **Roediger and Karpicke (2006).** Prose-learning experiments showing the importance of when the test takes place. [Test-enhanced learning experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/)
- **Pan and Rickard (2018).** Synthesizes tests of whether retrieval practice transfers to a new format or application. [Transfer meta-analysis](https://rickardlab.ucsd.edu/pdf/PR_2018.pdf)
- **Cepeda et al. (2006, 2008).** Meta-analysis of verbal recall and experiment on the relationship between spacing and the final retention interval. [Distributed practice meta-analysis](https://www.evullab.org/pdf/CepedaPashlerVulWixtedRohrer-PB-2006.pdf) · [Spacing interval study](https://laplab.ucsd.edu/articles/Cepeda%20et%20al%202008_psychsci.pdf)
- **Rawson and Dunlosky (2011), Latimier et al. (2021).** Study and synthesis on repeating successful retrieval across spaced sessions. [Retrieval schedules](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21707204/) · [Spaced retrieval meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09572-8)
- **Brunmair and Richter (2019).** Finds that the value of interleaving depends in part on the materials and similarity between them. [Similarity Matters](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31556629/)
- **Bisra et al. (2018), Sinha and Kapur (2021).** Reviews of prompted self-explanation and problem solving followed by explicit instruction. [Self-explanation](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9434-x) · [Productive failure](https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211019105)
- **Wisniewski et al. (2020).** Educational feedback synthesis that examines how the information in feedback relates to its effects. [The Power of Feedback Revisited](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087)
- **Bjork et al. (2013), Deslauriers et al. (2019).** Review of self-regulated learning and a classroom experiment comparing learners’ perceptions with observed learning. [Self-Regulated Learning](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-113011-143823) · [Actual versus perceived learning](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31484770/)

### Mathematics and programming

- **Barbieri et al. (2023).** Worked-example synthesis across elementary to postsecondary math. Correct examples had a positive average effect. Adding self-explanation prompts did not consistently improve outcomes. [Math worked examples meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09745-1)
- **Murray et al. (2025).** Math-specific synthesis supports spacing and finds uncertainty about the comparative retrieval advantage in a smaller set of studies. [Spacing and retrieval in mathematics](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-025-10035-1)
- **Rohrer et al. (2020).** Preregistered classroom experiment comparing blocked and interleaved seventh-grade math assignments. [Interleaved math practice](https://uweb.cas.usf.edu/~drohrer/pdfs/Rohrer_et_al_2020JEdPsych.pdf)
- **Rittle-Johnson et al. (2001), Rittle-Johnson and Star (2007).** Studies on how conceptual and procedural knowledge develop together and how strategy comparison can improve flexibility. [Conceptual and procedural knowledge](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.93.2.346) · [Compare solution methods](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.99.3.561)
- **Sentance et al. (2019), Ericson et al. (2022), Margulieux et al. (2020).** Sources on predicting and modifying code, rearranging solution lines, and labeling meaningful steps in examples. [PRIMM](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/229013/) · [Parsons problems review](https://doi.org/10.1145/3571785.3574127) · [Subgoal-labeled examples](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40594-020-00222-7)
- **Yang et al., Decoding Debugging Instruction.** Reviews debugging interventions, what they teach, and how evaluation varies. [Systematic literature review](https://doi.org/10.1145/3690652)

### Writing

- **Graham et al. (2025) and What Works Clearinghouse (2016).** Best-evidence reanalysis and guidance on secondary writing instruction. [Writing meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11145-024-10539-2) · [Teaching Secondary Students to Write Effectively](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/22)
- **Huisman et al. (2019), Cho and MacArthur (2011).** Higher-education synthesis of peer feedback and an experiment on learning by reviewing peers’ writing. [Formative peer feedback](https://doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2018.1545896) · [Learning by reviewing](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0021950)
- **Graham et al. (2020).** Meta-analysis of learning subject matter by writing about it across school subjects. [Writing to learn content](https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654320914744)

- **Luciani et al. (2022), Boucher et al. (2021).** Classical guitar research on motor and auditory feedback during learning and how video review affects self-evaluation. [Auditory feedback and motor learning](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9671877/) · [Video feedback in guitar](https://doi.org/10.1177/0305735619842374)
- **Duke et al. (2009), Carter and Grahn (2016), Mathias and Goldman (2025).** Studies of pianists’ practice choices and experimental comparisons of practice orders with clarinetists and violinists. [Piano practice and retention](https://doi.org/10.1177/0022429408328851) · [Clarinet practice order](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.01251) · [Violin practice order](https://doi.org/10.1177/00224294231222801)
- **Bergmann et al. (2021), Clemente et al. (2021), Beato et al. (2026).** Reviews and design guidance on soccer skills, coaching, and small-sided games. [Soccer skill acquisition](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.772201) · [Youth team sports interventions](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.667041) · [Soccer small-sided games consensus](https://researchonline.ljmu.ac.uk/id/eprint/28545/)
- **Video-based soccer training review and team-sport training meta-analysis.** Relevant to anticipation and decision practice, with different results across lab and game measures. [Soccer-specific video review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9686440/) · [Team-sport perceptual-cognitive training](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11505547/)

### AI tutoring and independence

- **Bastani et al. (2025).** Randomized high-school math field study with different modes of AI assistance and a later unaided test. [Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122)
- **Kestin et al. (2025).** Randomized trial of a designed tutor and in-class active learning in college physics. The measured learning gains were assessed immediately. [AI tutoring trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12179260/)
- **Shen and Tamkin (2026).** Preprint about developers learning an unfamiliar programming library, including the distinction between achieving the task and understanding it. [How AI Impacts Skill Formation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20245)
